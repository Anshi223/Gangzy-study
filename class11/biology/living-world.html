The Living World

Complete chapter notes, important concepts, quick revision and practice.

📖 Chapter Overview

Biology is the study of life and living organisms. The Living World introduces the basic characteristics of living organisms and explains why identification, nomenclature and classification are important in biology.

🧬 Characteristics of Living Organisms

1. Growth

Living organisms generally grow by increasing their cell number and/or cell size.

2. Reproduction

Living organisms produce new individuals. Reproduction may be sexual or asexual.

3. Metabolism

All living organisms have chemical reactions occurring inside their bodies. The sum of these reactions is called metabolism.

4. Cellular Organisation

Living organisms are made up of cells, which form the basic structural and functional units of life.

5. Consciousness

Living organisms can sense and respond to changes in their surroundings.

🌍 Biodiversity

The enormous variety of living organisms present on Earth is called biodiversity.

Why do we classify organisms?

  • To identify organisms easily
  • To organise biological information
  • To study similarities and differences
  • To understand relationships among organisms

Basic idea

Classification groups organisms according to their similarities and differences.

🏷️ Nomenclature

Nomenclature means giving scientific names to organisms according to internationally accepted rules.

Binomial Nomenclature

Binomial nomenclature gives each organism a two-word scientific name.

The first word represents the Genus and the second represents the specific epithet.

Example: Mangifera indica

🔬 Taxonomic Categories

Organisms are arranged into different hierarchical categories for systematic study.

Category Meaning
Species Basic unit of classification
Genus Group of related species
Family Group of related genera
Order Group of related families
Class Group of related orders
Phylum / Division Group of related classes
Kingdom Higher taxonomic category

📚 Taxonomic Aids

🌿

Herbarium

Collection of preserved plant specimens mounted on sheets for study and reference.

🌳

Botanical Gardens

Places where living plants are grown, maintained and labelled for study.

🐅

Museums

Collections of preserved specimens and other biological materials used for study.

🦁

Zoological Parks

Places where wild animals are kept in protected conditions for observation and study.

📖

Key

A taxonomic aid used for identifying organisms based on contrasting characteristics.

⚡ Quick Revision

  • Biology deals with the study of life and living organisms.
  • Important characteristics include growth, reproduction, metabolism, cellular organisation and consciousness.
  • Biodiversity refers to the variety of living organisms.
  • Scientific naming follows accepted rules of nomenclature.
  • Binomial nomenclature uses two words.
  • Species is the basic unit of classification.
  • Taxonomic aids help in identification and study of organisms.

❓ Practice Questions

Q1. What is biodiversity?

Answer: The variety of living organisms present on Earth is called biodiversity.

Q2. What is binomial nomenclature?

Answer: It is a system of scientific naming in which an organism is given a two-word scientific name.

Q3. What is the basic unit of classification?

Answer: Species.

Q4. What is a taxonomic key?

Answer: A key is a taxonomic aid used for identification of organisms using contrasting characteristics.
